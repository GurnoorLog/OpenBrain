## Business Overview

Apple Inc. is a multinational technology company that designs, manufactures,
and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and services.
Its core product segments are iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories,
and Services.

## Revenue Mix

Services is the fastest-growing segment, driven by the App Store, Apple Music,
iCloud, Apple TV+, and Apple Pay. Hardware remains the majority of revenue,
with iPhone as the single largest product line.

## Recent Strategic Directions

- Expansion of the services ecosystem to increase recurring revenue.
- Continued investment in on-device AI and machine learning capabilities.
- Supply chain diversification and increased vertical integration via custom
  silicon (Apple silicon).

## Analyst Observations

- High customer retention and a large installed base support recurring revenue.
- Regulatory scrutiny around app store practices is a key risk.
- Geopolitical trade tensions and component costs are watch items.
